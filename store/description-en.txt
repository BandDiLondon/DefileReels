DefileReels automatically moves to the next Facebook Reel when the current one ends. No more manual scrolling.

• A toolbar switch to turn auto-scroll on or off.
• Option to skip sponsored Reels.
• Works only on facebook.com Reels.
• Keeps going when the tab is in the background, and keeps the sound on from one Reel to the next.
• No data collected, no network requests, no cookies read: everything stays in your browser.
• Short, readable code, free software under GPL-3.0.

Transparency: code created with Claude (Anthropic), an AI, orchestrated by BandDiLondon, who defined the need and tested and approved every version.

DefileReels is an independent project, not affiliated with Facebook or Meta.
